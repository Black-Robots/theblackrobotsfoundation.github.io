BlackPDF

A free and open PDF viewer and editor that works offline.

Everything online PDF services do, but on your computer: merge, split, compress, convert, annotate, sign, protect and recognize text. Your documents never leave your computer.

Download for Windows
Download for Linux.deb package

Windows 10/11 and Linux · English, Spanish, Portuguese and French · Free software (AGPL-3.0) · All versions

Truly private

  • Offline: nothing is uploaded
  • No accounts, no ads
  • No telemetry
  • Open source

Everything in one application

Fast viewer

Tabs, thumbnails, outline, search, night mode and split view to compare documents.

Organize pages

Drag to reorder, rotate, duplicate, delete or insert pages from other PDFs.

PDF tools

Merge, split, alternate, extract, compress, watermark, page numbers, crop and resize.

Convert

PDF to PNG or JPG, images to PDF, and a PDF's pictures at their original quality. Also opens XPS and EPUB.

Annotate and comment

Pen, highlighter, shapes, notes and threaded comments, compatible with Acrobat.

Digital signature

Sign with your certificate (.pfx/.p12) with a visible stamp and timestamp. Verify the signatures of any PDF and comment on it without breaking them.

Built-in OCR

Turns scans into searchable, copyable text in four languages, with nothing else to install.

Security

Protect with 256-bit AES, remove passwords, permanently redact information and remove hidden data before sharing.

Attachments

See, open, save and add the files a PDF carries inside, such as a 3D part or a spreadsheet.

Measure drawings

Distances, perimeters and areas to scale, and layers you can show or hide.

Stamps

Approved, Draft, Reviewed... with your name and the date, or your own.

Tables to Excel

Turn a PDF's tables into Excel or CSV, with the numbers ready to add up.

3D models in PDF

Opens PDFs with U3D and PRC models from Acrobat, CAD software and LaTeX (Asymptote): rotate, cut, isolate parts and switch display modes. Measure like in a CAD program: it recognises corners, edges, circles and faces, and gives you diameters, distances between centres, thicknesses and angles. And put your own STEP, IGES, STL, OBJ or glTF parts into any PDF so everyone can see them in 3D.

Installation

Windows: run the installer; it does not need administrator rights and adds BlackPDF to "Open with". If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click "More info" and "Run anyway".

Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and open it, or install the .deb package on Debian, Ubuntu or Mint.
